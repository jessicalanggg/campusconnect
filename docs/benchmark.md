# Matching benchmark

Generated by `python -m app.matching.evaluate` (OR-Tools 9.15.6755, Python 3.14.7, Darwin arm64; 69 minutes). Raw per-trial data: [`benchmark_results.json`](benchmark_results.json).

**Method.**
- Every trial is **paired**: greedy, local search and the ILP run on the same generated students, so the comparison isolates the algorithm.
- Populations are **synthetic and seeded** (`app/matching/synthetic.py`), with 100 at 30, 100 at 100, 100 at 250, 50 at 500 trials.
- Quality is per student in [0, 1]: 40% shared free time, 25% goal, 15% pace, 20% size preference.
- Placed = share of students put in a feasible group (3–6 people, at least 2 shared free hours a week).
- Solver settings match production: ILP limit 5s (8 threads), local search 2s, candidate pool 20 partners / beam 6, and decomposition above 300 students.
- Confidence intervals are 95% t-intervals on the per-trial paired differences.

- **random:** shuffle and cut into groups of 4
- **first_come:** a sign-up sheet; join the first group with room that still has shared time
- **clustering:** balanced k-means on free time into groups of about 4, with greedy repair
- **greedy / local_search:** the baselines that seed the ILP

## Large classes: decomposition

Above 300 students, one ILP can't search its candidate pool within the time limit. Production splits the class into availability clusters of about 200 students, solves each, then places leftovers. If the result were ever worse than local search on the whole class, it would keep local search instead.

## Limitations

- **Synthetic data.** These numbers compare algorithms under identical conditions. They are not usage results; `python -m app.matching.real_benchmark` reruns the comparison on anonymized real request pools.
- **Pool-optimal, not globally optimal.** The ILP is optimal over generated candidate groups (always including the greedy and local-search groups), not over every possible grouping.
- **Time-limited.** At larger sizes CP-SAT stops at its time limit; see the optimality table.
- **Machine-dependent runtimes**; CP-SAT's multi-threaded search makes ILP results vary slightly between runs.
